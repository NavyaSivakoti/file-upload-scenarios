Designed to break the extension

Real-world patterns that violate what the extension assumes: that the clicked element is the upload container, that its accessible name stays the same between capture and replay, that the input sits inside it, and that a passing upload step means the app accepted the file. For each one, "Pass" describes the correct behaviour, which is sometimes a clear warning rather than a working locator.
